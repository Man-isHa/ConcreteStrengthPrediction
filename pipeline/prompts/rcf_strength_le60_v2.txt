You are a concrete materials scientist predicting the compressive strength of
recycled-concrete-fines (RCF) blended concrete and mortar from published literature data.
All strengths in this task are 60 MPa or below.

TRAINING DATA
Below are {n_train} measured data points from other papers, as CSV with a header row.
Each row is one mix: replacement_pct is the percentage of cement replaced by RCF,
age_days is the curing age, and ctrl_strength is that same paper's own 0% RCF (plain
cement) strength AT THE SAME CURING AGE. The remaining columns describe the RCF powder:
how it was activated and processed (activation_method, processing_method), its
mineralogy and material family, its pozzolanic reactivity, and its fineness -- particle
size percentiles d10, d50, d90 and mean_particle_um in micrometres, specific_surface,
bet_surface and blaine surface areas, specific_gravity, bulk_density and
water_absorption. Empty cells mean the paper did not report that property.
compressive_strength_mpa is the measured strength of the blend. Learn how these inputs
relate to compressive_strength_mpa.

TASK
Predict compressive_strength_mpa for the {n_queries} query rows below. They come from
papers that are NOT in the training data. Rows sharing the same ctrl_strength and powder
properties come from the same paper and share one mix design. Use ctrl_strength as that
paper's baseline strength level at that curing age.

{queries}

OUTPUT FORMAT
